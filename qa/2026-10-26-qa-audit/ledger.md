## Acceptance boundary

Do not call the product fully verified while a failed or unverified row remains. In particular:
- F1–F3 are measured detector defects; the handoff says the next work is the face, so they are retained as failures rather than silently redesigning the detector.
- A/B/C/D/K filter compliance, complete preset operations, visibility semantics, M/S linking, and meter timing need implementation or a separately authorized scope change.
- Host checks and real native input require a test path that meets the hidden-window preference and supports reliable input.
- No public visibility, upload, release increment, signing, or AAX work was performed.
